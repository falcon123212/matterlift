How to use MatterLift

  1. Ask. Type the material you want, or press 🎤 Speak. ✨ Enrich makes the request precise (colour, finish, wear). Add a photo to copy a real surface.
  2. Check. With a photo, MatterLift first shows what it understood: the surface SAM masked and the square it sampled. Then turn the sphere under Raking light and look for seams in Tiling 3×3.
  3. Export. Download .zip: PBR maps, a glTF .glb, Godot, Blender, MaterialX and USD files.

A material takes about 40 s at 512 px. The first one after a pause takes a few seconds more while the server wakes up.
